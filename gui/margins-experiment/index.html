Real.
Concentrated.

Federer’s point advantage was real. Its distribution matters.

The entire net fourteen-point surplus sits in a single, 38-point set. Across the other four sets, the total was even. Within those four, opposing margins cancel.

That does not erase the second set. Federer also won more games, 36–32. It does mean the full-match point total alone cannot establish sustained dominance.

The evidence.
Its limits.

This is a distribution of recorded points, not a verdict on who deserved to win. It cannot establish intent, nerves or what would have happened without set two. Tiebreaks are not all consequential points: Federer’s two championship points sit outside that group.
