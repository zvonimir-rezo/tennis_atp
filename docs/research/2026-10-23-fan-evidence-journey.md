## Bounded intervention

Central question: Did both ways forward work—and what does 'worked' mean?

Insight: Djokovic won all 11 charted serve-and-volley points in set two, versus one of five service points containing his own drop shot. The sole winning service drop-shot point was a counter-drop. A point outcome is not a stroke outcome; selection and different situations prevent a causal ranking of tactics.

Preserve existing reveal, service test, portraits, court split, pressure shots and source limits. Replace the remote generic source selector with a coherent comparison ledger between split and pressure game. It inherits the same scope, exposes every record in both compared groups, shows score before point and explicit winner, explains inclusion from actual shot tokens, and opens complete original fields.
